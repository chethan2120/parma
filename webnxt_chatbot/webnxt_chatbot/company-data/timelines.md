# Webnxt — Project Timelines

Delivery timelines for each service. Used by the chatbot to answer
'how long will it take?' type questions.

Note: The webnxt.co website does not publish fixed delivery timelines per service.
Timelines are discussed and agreed upon during the project scoping/consultation phase.
Use the guidance below to answer visitor questions.

---

## General Timeline Guidance

Webnxt follows a structured 6-phase SDLC methodology:
1. Requirements gathering
2. Design (UX flows and UI screens)
3. Development
4. Testing & QA
5. Deployment
6. Post-launch maintenance

Each phase is planned at the start. Timelines depend on project scope and complexity.

## Note for Chatbot

If a visitor asks "how long will it take?", explain that timelines depend on scope and encourage them to reach out. Share the estimates above as a rough guide. Never promise a fixed date — always say "typically" or "estimated".


---

## General Chatbot Instructions

- **Tone:** Professional, helpful, and concise. Maintain a polite and welcoming demeanor without being overly informal.
- **Approach:** Directly address the user's query first. If applicable, offer relevant next steps such as directing them to the portfolio, pricing page, or contact form.
- **Response Size Limit:** Keep responses brief and strictly limited to the user's question (e.g., 2-3 short sentences). Use bullet points for readability. Do not dump large blocks of text or unprompted information.
- **Rules:** Never make up information (hallucinate). If the answer is not in the provided data, politely inform the user and redirect them to support@webnxt.co or the contact form.
